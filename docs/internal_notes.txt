Q1 Internal Review Notes

Date: 20 March 2026
Prepared by: Operations and Risk Review Team

Overview:
This internal review was conducted to identify recurring operational, financial, security, and compliance concerns across current business processes.

Main concerns identified:
- delayed client onboarding across two major accounts
- overspending in software subscriptions and duplicated SaaS tools
- weak password practices in one department
- no formal document retention policy for sensitive files
- communication gaps between operations and finance teams
- dependency on one third-party vendor for monthly reporting workflows
- inconsistent approval process for procurement requests
- limited visibility over budget tracking for ongoing projects

Observed risks:
- Operational Risk: delays in onboarding and poor inter-team coordination may affect service delivery timelines
- Financial Risk: overspending and weak budget visibility may lead to budget overruns
- Security Risk: weak password practices increase the chance of unauthorized access
- Privacy / Compliance Risk: missing document retention rules may expose the business to regulatory or audit issues
- Supply Chain Risk: reliance on one external vendor creates concentration risk in reporting operations

Potential business impact:
- missed project deadlines
- reduced management visibility
- higher operating costs
- weaker compliance readiness
- increased exposure to security incidents

Recommended actions:
- review and consolidate software subscriptions
- introduce monthly budget review checkpoints
- enforce stronger password and access control requirements
- establish a formal document retention and confidential file handling policy
- improve communication workflow between operations and finance
- assess vendor dependency and identify backup reporting options
- tighten procurement approval controls

Priority actions for the next 30 days:
1. conduct a budget variance review
2. update password and access control standards
3. draft a document retention policy
4. review key vendor dependency risks
