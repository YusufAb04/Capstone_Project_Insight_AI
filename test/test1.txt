3. Reflection
You need to read the feedback written for you by your peers and reflect on this. When reflecting, you are encouraged to think about:
1.	What have you learnt from working on this project so far?
2.	Whether you think the reviewers’ comments are fair or not, and provide reason.
3.	Whether you think the reviewers’ comments are useful or not, and provide reason.
4.	How would you take the reviewers’ comments to improve your work/process?
5.	Comment on teamwork: How can your team collaborate better? How can you work better with your team members? How can you influence your team members to work better as a team?

	Answer:

1.	Through this project, I have learned a great deal about working effectively in a team environment, including how to communicate clearly, divide responsibilities, and stay aligned toward a shared goal. I also developed stronger time management skills, learning how to balance my individual contributions with the demands of the overall project timeline.
2.	I believe the peer review process is fair, as it provides an outside perspective that we may not always be able to see in ourselves. Reviewers can observe how we contribute to the team, communicate, and carry out our responsibilities from a different vantage point, which makes the feedback more well-rounded and objective.
3.	The reviewers’ comments are useful, as they highlight areas of strength and areas for growth that can directly inform how we approach our work going forward. Receiving structured feedback from peers helps build self-awareness and encourages continuous improvement throughout the project.
4.	I would take the reviewers’ comments on board by actively reflecting on the areas they have identified and setting small, achievable goals to address them. Rather than viewing the feedback as criticism, I would treat it as an opportunity to refine my approach and become a more effective contributor to the team.
5.	As a team, we could collaborate more effectively by establishing clearer communication norms, such as regular check-ins and shared progress updates. On my part, I can contribute by being more proactive in reaching out to team members, offering support early, and fostering a culture where everyone feels comfortable sharing ideas and raising concerns openly.
